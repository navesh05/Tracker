# Changelog

## 4.3.0
- Refined luxury-minimal light/dark visual system.
- Today is explicitly editable; future dates remain blocked while past dates remain editable.
- Home Gym and Run status cards are clickable and use condition-aware colors; removed redundant Open Gym link.
- Normalized training plan schedules with explicit plan period.
- Reworked nutrition plan presentation to remove empty-looking rows and show all structured data.


- Monthly activity heatmap replaces the long rolling heatmap.
- Removed non-functional 7d/30d activity toggle.
- Future Gym/Run dates are view-only and repository-level validation rejects future activity.
- Reworked Gym/Run logging dialogs into radio/checkbox cards.
- Removed weight from Daily Tracker.
- Added weekly weight check-ins with plan targets and historical recovery when a day is missed.
- Added IndexedDB weight history, migration, validation, sync queue and Firebase sync.
- Added workbook-backed Training, Running, Nutrition and Weight Target tables under Suggestions & Plan.
- Added full 20-week running progression and richer gym/diet plan data.
